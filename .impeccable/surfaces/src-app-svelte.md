# Surface: Taiwan trip scroller (single page)

Mode: Experience. The trip leads from the first viewport.
Audience: friends and family on phones via shared link; Alex and Ting as a keepsake. Desktop and mobile both designed.
Job: scroll the month as a journey; always know where on the island and where in the trip.
Content: legs from PRODUCT.md, mock photos (clearly placeholder) until real ones arrive; no dates yet.
Memorable moment: the zoom from island scale down to the Mianyue Line's own sheet at Alishan, the trip's highlight.
Unresolved: dates; subdomain; real photos and captions; Tainan/Kaohsiung legs once completed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The month plotted on one Taiwan topographic survey sheet. Contours, rivers and rail are printed, and our route is overprinted in vermilion as you scroll. It refuses the category default of a dark vector basemap on one side with a stack of floating photo cards on the other.

OWN-WORLD: The colours of an offset-printed survey sheet: pale survey-green land, brown contours thickening into the Central Range, blue water and coast, black hatched rail, and a single vermilion overprint for our route. Sheet-margin furniture (title block, scale bar, graticule ticks, sheet numbers, legend) is the UI. Photos sit as plates clipped onto the sheet with registration marks. Transport is drawn in legend symbols: rail hatching for train, a dashed line for bus, solid for car, a dotted footpath for walking.

STORY: A visitor sees Taiwan as one printed sheet. Scrolling inks the route place by place, and at each leg the sheet pans and zooms there while the plates and a short note come in. At Alishan the view drops to a large-scale sheet of the Mianyue Line and inks the trail on foot. They finish knowing where we went, how we got there, and what it looked like.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The whole island printed full-bleed with contour relief, the Central Range dominant. A survey title block in the lower corner: TAIWAN 台灣, 2026, our names, a scale bar in miles and a legend for the four transport symbols. A small vermilion start mark at Taipei, and the first scroll begins inking. On a phone the sheet is a window across the top 46% of the screen, island whole inside it, with the title block below. The story scrolls under the window so the map never hides behind text (changed during build for phone readability).

FORM: Survey Sheet. Impeccable's pick, ranked #1 on my grounded list. Seed 232636eb.
